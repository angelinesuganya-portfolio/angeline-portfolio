About me

I am an architectural researcher specialising in traditional Islamic architecture, Mashrabiya, passive cooling, environmental design, and craft heritage. My research explores how traditional architectural knowledge can inform contemporary approaches to climate-responsive and sustainable design.

My work combines architectural research, environmental simulation, experimentation, fieldwork, and research-through-design. I am particularly interested in the relationship between architecture, climate, materiality, craftsmanship, and cultural heritage, with a focus on reinterpreting traditional passive strategies for contemporary applications.

What i'm doing

  • Architectural Research

    Researching traditional Islamic architecture, environmental design, passive cooling, and climate-responsive strategies.

  • Environmental Design

    Exploring the relationship between architecture, climate, energy, comfort, and building performance through simulation and experimental methods.

  • Craft & Heritage

    Documenting Mashrabiya, traditional woodworking, craftsmen, and the knowledge embedded within historic architectural traditions.

  • Research Through Design

    Developing and testing innovative architectural systems inspired by traditional environmental principles.
