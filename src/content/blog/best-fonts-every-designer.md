Typography quietly carries the weight of every interface. The right typeface
sets tone, guides the eye, and earns trust long before a single word is read.

This shortlist favors versatile families with broad weights and strong legibility
at small sizes. Pair a sturdy text face with a characterful display face and most
projects are well served.
