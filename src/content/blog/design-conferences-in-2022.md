The year ahead brings a packed calendar of design gatherings, from intimate
local meetups to sprawling international summits. Each promises a chance to
trade ideas, sharpen craft, and meet the people shaping the field.

Whether you attend for the talks, the workshops, or the hallway conversations,
planning early pays off. Block the dates, budget for travel, and decide which
sessions matter most before tickets sell out.
