Whitespace is not empty space; it is structure. Generous, consistent spacing
lets content breathe, signals grouping, and turns a cramped layout into a calm,
readable one.

Build a simple scale and stick to it. When everything aligns to the same rhythm,
even a dense screen feels intentional rather than crowded.
