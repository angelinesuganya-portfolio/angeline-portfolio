Small motion choices make interfaces feel alive. This week we collected
micro-interactions that clarify state, reward attention, and add a touch of
delight without getting in the way.

Notice how the strongest examples stay subtle: timing is quick, easing is
natural, and every animation has a clear job. Motion for its own sake is the
fastest route to fatigue.
